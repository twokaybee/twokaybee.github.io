## Projects & Experience

### Tathkarah
**Title:** Global Booking System and Distributed Microservices Architecture

I managed the complete replacement of a monolithic legacy Global Distribution System. I engineered a distributed platform of eighteen independent services optimized to handle massive concurrency and unstable network conditions. I successfully executed the migration in phases without any system downtime or data loss.

To ensure system stability and speed up developer onboarding, I set the technical roadmap and Go engineering standards for the entire development lifecycle. I mentored independent engineering teams and technical leads, enforcing clean concurrency patterns and treating foundational code as internal open source.

I wrote the foundational Go shared libraries, middleware, and core services. I architected an asynchronous messaging fabric using NATS and implemented low overhead inter service communication via gRPC and Protobuf, keeping services completely decoupled for maximum team autonomy.

Furthermore, I architected the Backend Console, a centralized control plane driven by a custom Business Process Management engine. I engineered a secure finite state machine to remove hardcoding from the environment entirely. This empowered business operations to dynamically publish configurations directly to the core booking service utilizing MongoDB and Redis cache.

To support massive data loads, I designed a high throughput ETL aggregation service to concurrently process, normalize, and cache heavy flight data across massive supplier ecosystems including Travelport, Galileo, Rezlive, and Juniper, alongside direct airline API integrations.

Ultimately, this architecture sustained massive real time booking volumes while keeping latency strictly under one second. It dramatically reduced engineering overhead by separating core business logic from deployment cycles, shifting operational control to stakeholders while keeping the engineering team focused on scale.

**Stack:** Golang, Kubernetes, Microservices, NATS, PostgreSQL
